**Character:** Hank Delgado, @lawndoctorhank, about 56, retired golf-course groundskeeper from outside Nashville. Teaches "golf-course results on a hardware-store budget." Catchphrase: *"Feed the soil, not the weeds."* AI-generated character; disclose it on every post and listing.
**Assets already built:** approved character sheet (5e9e3174), trained Soul ID, profile.

## Honest odds first

$10,000 in 12 weeks from a brand-new account, in lawn care's **off-season**, is a stretch goal, not a forecast.
- Most new faceless or AI creators make **$0–500** in their first 3 months.
- Homeowners buy lawn products in **spring**, and October–December is when lawn traffic is lowest.
- To reach $10k on a $19 calendar alone, you'd need about **590 sales**.

So this plan does two things:
1. It shifts Hank's main buyer from homeowners to **lawn care business owners**. Their slow season is now, they plan and buy business tools in Nov–Feb, and they pay $47–97 for tools that make them money.
2. It gives the target in three tiers, so you know where you are each week.

| Tier | Dec 31 total | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Floor | $300–1,000 | Etsy listings live by Oct 20, posting daily |
| Good | $2,500–4,000 | One Reel breaks 100k views and the business kit sells steadily |
| Stretch ($10k) | $10,000 | Several viral posts, about 60 business kits, about 200 calendars, plus affiliate income |
