1. Post at 11am–1pm or 7–9pm (your audience's time).
2. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
3. Leave 5–10 genuine comments on bigger Halloween/DIY accounts.
4. Check views, saves and shares weekly — make more of whatever wins.
