## Money math
- Etsy: no monthly fee; about $0.20/listing, 6.5% transaction fee, ~3% + $0.25 payment processing (check Etsy's current fees).
- About $6.50 kept per $7.50 sale → ~46 sales for $300. Bundle sales get there faster.
- Achievable with 10–15 good listings in Nov–Dec, but not guaranteed.
