🎃 The No-Stress Halloween Checklist

Print it, stick it on the fridge, check it off. — Holly Bramble, @hollydaysathome

4 weeks out

  • Raid the house: old sheets, jars, sticks, black clothes
  • Set a budget (I do $50 total)
  • Pick costumes and check what's already in the closet
  • Ask neighbors for extra pumpkins

3 weeks out

  • One shopping trip, list only
  • Buy lights, cheesecloth and paint, not big decor
  • Start any DIY that needs drying time

2 weeks out

  • Finish the costumes and try them on
  • Make the paper bats and window silhouettes
  • Plan one easy snack and a drink

Final week

  • Hang the lights and porch decor
  • Set up one "wow" spot (porch or mantel)
  • Buy candy, no earlier than 3 days before
  • Check batteries in tea lights and flashlights

Halloween day

  • Start the cider in the afternoon
  • Test the lights at dusk
  • Porch light ON, and leave it on late
  • Save the good candy for the last knock
  • Take one photo, then put the phone down

November 1st

  • Shop the 50–90% off sales for next year
  • Pack everything into one labeled bin
